# ZeroOS wallpaper prompts

Prompts for the built-in wallpapers (F6.8). Every prompt is complete: copy one code block, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT, and save the image.

## How to use

1. Copy the whole code block under **Dark** or **Light** for a design.
2. Paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT.
3. Download the image (PNG if offered) and save it in `docs/brand/wallpapers/` with the file name shown above the block.

If a result is too bright or busy, reply in the same chat:

```text
Make it sparser: fewer and smaller dots, more empty space, keep it dark.
```

If the dots look soft or grey, reply:

```text
Redo it with crisp, separate pure white dots on pure black, no grey, no blur.
```

For light versions, swap white and black, and dark and light, in those replies.

## 1. Constellation

Seven stars joined by dotted lines into an imagined swan in flight, over a few faint stars.

### Constellation: Dark

Save as `wallpaper_constellation_dark.png`

```text
Create an image: an artistic, minimal phone wallpaper in a dot-matrix halftone style.

Subject: A quiet night-sky constellation. Seven stars of different sizes, the largest about 30 px across and the smallest about 10 px, are joined into one graceful open shape, like an imagined constellation of a swan in flight, by connecting lines that are themselves rows of tiny white dots with clear gaps between them, not solid lines. The constellation fills roughly the middle half of the image width, centred, between 32% and 62% of the image height. Around it, a very sparse sprinkling of about 25 tiny pin-prick dots (3 to 5 px) suggests distant stars, scattered unevenly across the whole image but never clustered.

Art style: the entire image is made of separate, perfectly round dots with crisp edges, like a fine halftone print, stipple engraving or LED dot-matrix display. There are no solid lines and no filled shapes: outlines are rows of dots, and light and shade come only from the size and spacing of the dots (bigger, closer dots read brighter; smaller, sparser dots read darker). Elegant, refined and calm, with plenty of empty space, like a piece of minimalist art rather than clip art.

Colours: only two colours. The background is one completely flat, uniform pure black (#000000) from edge to edge, and every dot is solid pure white (#FFFFFF). No grey tones, no in-between shades anywhere.

Keep it dark: the white dots together cover no more than about 4% of the whole image, and the rest is empty pure black. No large or dense white areas: even the brightest parts are separate dots with clear black gaps between them, so the wallpaper stays dark and easy on the eyes.

Size and layout: tall portrait phone wallpaper, 9:20 aspect ratio, 1260 x 2800 pixels. If 9:20 isn't possible, use 9:16 portrait and keep the subject inside the centre 70% of the width. The top 30% and the bottom 30% of the image stay empty (apart from a few tiny scattered dots where the subject asks for them), because a clock sits at the top and app names and a fingerprint sensor sit at the bottom.

Do not add: colour, grey, gradients, glow, bloom, blur, soft focus, shadows, 3D rendering, reflections, texture, noise, film grain, vignette, haze, sparkles, borders or frames around the image, text, letters, numbers, watermarks, signatures, logos, UI elements, phone mockups or device frames.

Output only the finished image, nothing else.
```
